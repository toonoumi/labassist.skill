# Base (box-speed) development times

All times are at **20°C (68°F)**, standard periodic agitation (see
`agitation.md`), rolled 35mm/120 film on a reel in a standard tank. These are
commonly published starting points (Massive Dev Chart / manufacturer
datasheets) — treat them as a baseline, not gospel. Individual results vary
with thermometer accuracy, exact reel loading, water chemistry, and personal
agitation habits. When in doubt, or for a combo not listed here, say so and
point the user to the Massive Dev Chart (digitaltruth.com) rather than
inventing a number.

Times are `mm:ss`.

## If the combo isn't in this table

Don't guess a precise number with false confidence. Give the user the
closest analogous film/developer pair as a rough anchor, clearly flag it as
an estimate, and suggest they cross-check the Massive Dev Chart app or the
manufacturer's datasheet before committing a whole roll — B&W has latitude,
but there's no reason to fly blind when better data exists.
