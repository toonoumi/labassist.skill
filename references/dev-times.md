# Base (box-speed) development times

All times are at **20°C (68°F)**, standard periodic agitation (see
`agitation.md`), rolled 35mm/120 film on a reel in a standard tank. These are
commonly published starting points (Massive Dev Chart / manufacturer
datasheets) — treat them as a baseline, not gospel. Individual results vary
with thermometer accuracy, exact reel loading, water chemistry, and personal
agitation habits. When in doubt, or for a combo not listed here, say so and
point the user to the Massive Dev Chart (digitaltruth.com) rather than
inventing a number.

Times are `mm:ss`. Entries marked **(approx)** are lower-confidence —
less-common developers with more source-to-source variance. Say so if you
quote one.

## Special-case developers — read before quoting

- **Diafine** (two-bath): times are essentially fixed regardless of film
  speed or temperature within its working range (21-29°C) — that's the
  point of the developer. Bath A ~3 min, Bath B ~3 min, brief agitation
  (a few inversions) at the start of each minute in each bath, no rinse
  between baths (just drain). Diafine is also famous for giving most
  films an effective 1-2 stop "free" speed boost with little grain
  penalty — see `push-pull.md`.
- **Pyrocat-HD** (staining pyro developer): times above are **approx** —
  real-world times vary a lot with agitation style (many users run it at
  reduced/semi-stand agitation) and personal taste for stain density.
  Treat these as a rough starting point only, and say so.
- **Perceptol**: a fine-grain, speed-reducing developer — normal practice
  is to shoot the film at half box speed (e.g. HP5+ at EI 200) to get the
  grain benefit, not to push it.

## If the combo isn't in this table

Don't guess a precise number with false confidence. Give the user the
closest analogous film/developer pair as a rough anchor, clearly flag it as
an estimate, and suggest they cross-check the Massive Dev Chart app or the
manufacturer's datasheet before committing a whole roll — B&W has latitude,
but there's no reason to fly blind when better data exists.
